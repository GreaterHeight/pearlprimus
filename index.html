Capability Areas

Four commercial capability domains

Pearl Primus operates across four capability areas — each constituting a distinct commercial service offering for Nigerian government, private sector, and institutional clients.

📋

Contract Administration

End-to-end contract management for Nigerian government and private sector clients — from tender response preparation through award, execution oversight, variation management, and final account settlement. We manage the administrative and commercial lifecycle of contracts so clients and principals can focus on delivery.

Tender PreparationContract ManagementVariation OrdersFinal AccountsBPP Compliance

Project Facilitation

Project facilitation for complex, multi-party Nigerian projects — coordinating stakeholders, managing project interfaces, navigating regulatory requirements, and ensuring delivery against scope, budget, and timeline. Particularly suited to government-funded and donor-funded project environments where coordination complexity is highest.
