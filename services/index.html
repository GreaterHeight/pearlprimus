Four capability domains

Pearl Primus operates across four capability areas — contract administration, project facilitation, investment management, and commercial ventures — serving government, private sector, and institutional clients across Nigeria.

01
Government · Private Sector · Institutions

Contract Administration

End-to-end contract management for Nigerian government and private sector clients — from pre-contract documentation through execution oversight, variation management, claims resolution, and final account settlement. Our contract administration practice is built around the BPP framework and Nigerian public procurement regulations for government contracts, with equivalent rigour applied to private sector engagements.

We prepare, review, and manage contract documentation; coordinate between client and contractor; administer payment certifications; manage contract variations; and maintain the complete contract record — ensuring that every contractual obligation is documented, tracked, and enforced.

02

Project Facilitation

Project facilitation for complex, multi-party Nigerian projects — coordinating stakeholders, managing project interfaces, navigating regulatory requirements, and ensuring delivery against scope, budget, and timeline. Pearl Primus provides an experienced facilitation layer between project principals, contractors, government authorities, and end users.

Our facilitation practice is particularly suited to government-funded and donor-funded project environments — where procurement complexity, multiple accountability structures, and regulatory requirements create coordination challenges that specialist facilitation can resolve efficiently.

Investment Management

Commercial investment management for contracting and venture opportunities — structuring joint ventures, managing investment participation in contracts, and creating commercially structured models for investors seeking exposure to Nigerian contracting markets without direct operational involvement.

Pearl Primus acts as the contracting and management principal in structured investment arrangements — providing operational execution capability while delivering returns to investment partners through agreed commercial structures.

Commercial Ventures

Structured commercial ventures — supply, distribution, and commercial facilitation within Nigerian markets. Pearl Primus identifies and executes commercial opportunities that leverage our contracting expertise, market access, and OA Group relationships to create value in Nigerian commercial markets.
