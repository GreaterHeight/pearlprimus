Pearl Primus›Sectors

Five sectors. One contracting firm.

Pearl Primus delivers contract administration, project facilitation, and commercial services across five sector domains — each with distinct procurement frameworks, compliance requirements, and delivery characteristics.

🏛️

Government

Federal and state government procurement is the largest contracting sector in Nigeria, governed by the Bureau of Public Procurement (BPP) framework, the Public Procurement Act 2007, and sector-specific regulations. Pearl Primus is BPP registered and maintains all documentation required for government prequalification.

We have experience with Federal Ministry and parastatal procurement processes, state government contracting, and competitive tender procedures — enabling rapid and compliant response to expressions of interest and invitation to tender across government tiers.

View Our Government Prequalification
⛽

Oil & Gas Support

Nigeria's oil and gas sector generates significant contracting activity — from upstream operations support through midstream logistics and downstream distribution. Pearl Primus provides contract administration and project facilitation for oil and gas support services, supply chain management, and ancillary service provision to E&P companies, OML operators, and service firms.

Our engagements in this sector focus on the support service and supply chain layers — not direct field operations — enabling rapid mobilisation without sector-specific technical licensing requirements.

🏗️

Construction

Construction project facilitation and contract administration for developers, institutional contractors, and construction managers — coordinating contractor appointments, managing subcontract documentation, and administering payment certifications and final accounts for residential, commercial, and infrastructure construction projects.

We work with both private sector developers and government construction agencies — applying the appropriate procurement framework (BPP for public projects, standard commercial frameworks for private) to each engagement.

🌍

NGO & Development

International NGOs, donor agencies, and development finance institutions operating in Nigeria — including UN agencies, World Bank project management units, bilateral donors, and international development organisations — have structured procurement requirements that differ materially from government BPP and private sector frameworks.

Pearl Primus provides project facilitation for NGO and development sector engagements — managing procurement processes, coordinating local stakeholders, maintaining project documentation, and reporting against donor requirements.

🏢

Commercial

Private sector commercial contracting, supply arrangements, and commercial ventures — structured around the commercial requirements of the specific engagement rather than a regulatory procurement framework. Our commercial sector work includes supply contract administration, distribution arrangements, and joint venture facilitation across Nigerian markets.
